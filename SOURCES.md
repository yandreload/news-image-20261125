Every story on the front page was reported on 25 November 2026, or

## Lead — Shenzhou-24, first Pakistani in orbit (Wed 25 Nov)
- Reuters, 25 Nov 2026: China's Shenzhou-24 lifted off with a
  Pakistani national as the first foreign astronaut — Long March-2F
  launched 00:18 GMT from Jiuquan; Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, 40, payload
  specialist, five to ten days aboard Tiangong.
- Xinhua and China Daily Asia, 25 Nov: the first joint flight of
  Chinese and foreign astronauts in China's crewed space programme;
  crew Tang Hongbo, Wu Yi and Muhammad Zeeshan Ali.
- The News (Pakistan), 25 Nov: Ali to run four Pakistani biotech
  experiments in orbit.

## Colombia — Cartagena hostages (Tue 24 Nov)
- Associated Press, 24 Nov: armed men seized an Éxito supermarket
  in Cartagena on Tuesday, holding at least 25 hostages; President
  Abelardo De La Espriella said they were "common criminals" after
  a failed armoured-truck robbery; 30 were initially held and at
  least five elderly women were released.
- Reuters, 24 Nov: 20 still held after 10 were released;
  negotiations under way. CBS News, 24 Nov: the standoff continued
  into the night, with hostage negotiators in direct talks.
